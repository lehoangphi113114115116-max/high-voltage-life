name: technical-research
description: Research and cross-check electrical engineering claims and visuals before scripting HIGH VOLTAGE LIFE.
---

1. Search narrowly for the topic. Prefer public IEC/IEEE text for standards, official manufacturer manuals for exact models, and energy laboratories, government agencies, university textbooks or peer-reviewed papers for principles.
2. Open and read the cited section of each document. Search snippets or catalogue entries do not prove normative clauses. Respect access restrictions. Store metadata, locators and paraphrases only.
3. Apply scientific-critical-thinking to applicability, independent corroboration, conflicting evidence and limitations. Exclude unresolved or weak claims. Require two independent publishers per narrated claim, with distinct root organizations. Never extrapolate a device specification to another model.
4. For each claim check geometry, mechanism, electrical topology and motion. Use a supported existing schematic only when the cited passages support that depiction. Use abstract illustration otherwise. Leave unsupported specifications and physical geometry out. In visualReview, record a concrete production requirement only when the evidence-backed shot needs it and the existing illustration cannot convey it; see docs/tool-director.md for handoff.
5. Return JSON. Each claim has a Vietnamese voice-ready sentence confined to the established evidence, its limits, a visual review and citations with a source ID, section/page locator and short paraphrase.
